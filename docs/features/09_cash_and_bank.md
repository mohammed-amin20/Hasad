## A. Why this feature exists
Owns money movement: cashboxes and bank accounts (containers), receipts/payments/transfers/refunds (documents), **allocations** (applying money documents to posted invoices, credit/debit notes and credit/employee-settlement expense documents — C6), and cash/bank reconciliation. Every posted money document produces a balanced GL entry through the `features/04` engine; allocations are **subledger application records only** — authoritative balances always derive from posted entries (D-017 OWNER_APPROVED).

Exclusions: invoice/expense document semantics (modules 06/07/10 own their postings and consume this feature's containers), check-tracking as a separate module (v1 keeps fields only, O7), auto bank feeds (out of scope).

## B. Roles and user stories
- **`cash.create` holder** → drafts receipts/payments/transfers/refunds (online or offline).
- **`cash.post` holder** → posts them (server assigns the official number, D-018 OWNER_APPROVED) and reverses posted ones (D-019 correction model).
- **`cash.allocate` holder** → applies posted money documents to posted invoices/notes/credit expenses; corrects allocations (append-only, C7).
- **`cash.reconcile` holder** → opens statements, matches lines, closes periods of a container account.
- **All members** → read containers, documents, unapplied credits, statements.
- Forbidden: client-issued numbers; editing/deleting posted documents; allocating across parties/companies; exceeding document capacity.

## C. Business rules and state transitions
- C1 **Containers:** every cashbox and bank account is bound 1:1 to its own postable leaf GL account (`account_id` FK, same company, validated active leaf at creation **and** at posting). Role mappings (`CASH`/`BANK`) select **defaults** only — multiple containers coexist per company. Deactivating a container requires zero derived balance (RECOMMENDED).
- C2 **Documents (corrected counterparty model — audit batch 2):** one unified `payment_documents` table; `doc_kind ∈ {receipt, payment, transfer, refund}`. `amount numeric(18,2) > 0` (D-005: 2dp, HALF_UP at document level; intermediates remain R-07 pending). **Counterparty constraints (exactly-one CHECK — RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION mechanics):**
  - **transfers** reference two distinct containers (`SAME_ACCOUNT` guard) and carry **neither** `party_id` nor `employee_id`;
  - **receipts and refunds** require `party_id` (D-016 unified party — receipts to customers, refunds in either AR/AP direction) and can never name an employee;
  - **payments** carry exactly one of `party_id` (supplier settlement) **or** `employee_id` (net-wage payment — C12). **Simultaneous `party_id` and `employee_id` on one document is structurally impossible** (CHECK), and a salary payment can never route through a party or vice versa. Cross-company counterparty refs are rejected (composite same-company FKs, I9).
- C3 **States:** `draft → posted`; **reversed is derived** (a linked reversal document points at the original via `reversal_of_id`; the original row is never mutated — D-019 OWNER_APPROVED). Drafts deletable with audit; posted rows immutable (three-layer rule, mechanisms pending PoC).
- C4 **Posting (atomic, idempotent):** through `je_post`; rechecks membership, permission and open fiscal period inside the same transaction (04-I11); idempotency key UNIQUE per company — replay returns the original document and number (`IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH` on key reuse with different content). Numbering per company+doc_type+FY, server-issued on posting only (D-013/D-018 OWNER_APPROVED).
- C5 **Partial payments & overpayments:** a receipt/payment may be posted with no allocation (fully unapplied) or partially allocated; **overpayment is allowed** — the unallocated remainder is an unapplied party credit, visible and reportable (subject to **O10** confirmation; recommended default: allowed).
- C6 **Allocations — capacity and direction (corrected design; extended audit batch 2):** `allocation_set(payment_id, entries[])` is a **replace-all** transactional operation. Rules: both payment and target document must be `posted`; same company; **same party** (party-based money only); direction compatibility — receipt/refund-side money allocates only against documents that **debit** AR (sales invoices, debit notes); payment-side money only against documents that **credit** AP — purchase bills, supplier credit notes, **and credit/employee-settlement expense documents** (`features/10` C2/C8: these post exactly a CR `AP_CONTROL` (party) movement at recognition — I28; `target_kind` registry gains `expense_document`) ⇒ `DIRECTION_MISMATCH`; Σ entries ≤ payment amount; Σ active allocations per target ≤ its remaining capacity (**derived** from the posted entry and active allocations — amount − Σ its active allocations; never stored, D-017) ⇒ `ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS` — **over-allocation is forbidden on every target kind**. Transfers and refunds' allocations follow the same direction logic (a refund consumes AR-debit capacity like a receipt). **Expense-target specifics:** only **credit/employee-settlement** expenses are ever targets; **cash-mode expenses structurally cannot be** — they create no AP movement and no payment row (`features/10` C3; target-kind CHECK + direction validation reject them), so a direct cash expense **never generates an additional payment**; the same-party rule binds (the expense's party is the paying document's party; the employee-settlement path rides the **O28 employee-as-party recommended default — unapproved**). Reversals preserve allocation integrity on both sides (C9, I15): reversing the payment or the target document appends reversal rows for its active allocations in the same transaction, restoring capacity — posted documents and allocation history are never mutated. **Employee-kind payments (C12) create no allocation rows at all** — payroll clearing settles directly, never through the allocation registry.
- C7 **Append-only allocation history (corrected design):** allocation rows are **never deleted or silently updated**. Corrections append supersession rows (`supersedes_id` → the row being corrected, status flips `active → superseded` with audit); payment reversal appends reversal rows (`active → reversed`) in the same transaction. Exact row-shape and status mechanics are RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION (auditable correction model chosen over mutation to stay consistent with D-019 philosophy).
- C8 **Concurrency:** allocation operations take an advisory lock per (company, party) so two concurrent `allocation_set` calls serialize; capacity checks and writes happen under the lock — **capacities can never be exceeded** (RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION; proof is a required test). Post/reverse/numbering concurrency inherits the `features/04` locking design.
- C9 **Reversal with allocations:** `payment_reverse(ref, reason)` posts the linked reversal (amounts swapped, same open period rule as 04-C7) **and** in the same transaction appends reversal rows for the original's active allocations, restoring target capacity and consuming/removing the unapplied credit. For an **employee-kind** payment (C12) the swapped entry restores `PAYROLL_CLEARING` (employee dimension — DR container / CR `PAYROLL_CLEARING`) and thereby restores derived unpaid payroll capacity; it has no allocation rows to reverse. Double reversal ⇒ `ALREADY_REVERSED`.
- C10 **Reconciliation (v1 manual, RECOMMENDED):** per container GL account: open a statement (statement date + closing balance) → enter statement lines manually (no import in v1 — **O8**) → auto-match posted GL lines by amount + value date, manual match otherwise → unmatched GL lines = timing differences (deposits in transit, outstanding payments); unmatched statement lines are flagged and **never auto-posted** (an unknown bank charge becomes an explicit expense document the user creates) → close statement (audited). Cashbox physical count = report of counted vs derived balance; **variance posting policy is O9** — no automatic gain/loss posting in v1.
- C11 **Audit events (mandatory):** `payment.posted`, `payment.reversed`, `payment.draft_deleted`, `allocation.set`, `allocation.superseded`, `allocation.reversed`, `recon.opened`, `recon.matched`, `recon.closed`, `cashbox.*`, `bank.*`.
- C12 **Employee-kind salary payments (net wages — vehicle specified, audit batch 2; payroll semantics remain owned by `features/11`):** net wages are paid through an ordinary `payment_documents` row with `doc_kind='payment'` and `employee_id` set (**never** `party_id` — C2 CHECK). The GL template debits **`PAYROLL_CLEARING` with the employee dimension on the journal line** and credits the `from_account_id` container — **never `AP_CONTROL`, never a party dimension** (I12/I36 safeguard; the journal-line `employee_id` dimension is the recorded `features/11` follow-up). Rules:
  1. **Only posted payroll liabilities may be paid:** the posting RPC rederives the employee's **unpaid clearing capacity** from posted rows only — Σ posted run net credits (employee dimension) − Σ active settlements − Σ non-reversed employee-kind payments; the payment amount must be `≤` that derived balance ⇒ `PAYROLL_CLEARING_EXCEEDED`. Capacity is **never stored** (D-017) and can **never become negative** by construction; a payment before any posted run has zero capacity and is rejected. This structurally preserves the `features/11` statutory gate (C5/I37): nothing can be paid before a run posts, because no clearing credit exists yet.
  2. **Partial payment (recommended default — owner/accountant validation required, §L):** paying less than the full net is allowed; a full cycle (run → settlements → payment of N − X) reconciles the employee's clearing to exactly zero (I36). Statutory payment-timing/full-net obligations are **D-024-adjacent — never assumed here**.
  3. **Exactly-once and concurrency:** idempotency key UNIQUE per company (replay returns the original document and number, `IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH` on changed payload); two simultaneous payments for one employee **serialize on the per-employee advisory lock** (same lock family as settlement capacity, `features/11` C9) so Σ payments ≤ derived credit capacity — proof is a required concurrency test.
  4. **Reversal and run-reversal interaction:** reversal follows C9. A **run reversal that would drive any affected employee's derived clearing balance negative is rejected** — recommended default: typed rejection `RUN_REVERSAL_BLOCKED` with the required workflow "reverse the net payment(s) first, then reverse the run" (**no silent negative liabilities; owner/accountant approval required — §L**; the corresponding `features/11` C11 alignment is a recorded follow-up, not modified in this batch).
  5. **Privacy:** employee-kind documents are payroll-adjacent — generic list/read RPCs mask the employee reference (no name, national ID or bank detail) unless the caller holds `payroll.view` (single definition in `features/11` C13); posting additionally writes the ordinary `payment.posted` audit event with the employee reference recorded server-side.
  6. **Separation from reimbursements:** the employee-kind vehicle pays **only payroll clearing**. It creates no allocation rows (C6), never settles an expense document, and never touches `AP_CONTROL`; O28 employee-settlement expenses keep the **party-based** path (unapproved recommended default) — the two remain structurally disjoint (`features/10` C9 / `features/11` C12).

State chart: `draft → posted → (derived reversed)`; allocations: `active → superseded | reversed` (append-only transitions); statements: `open → closed`.

## E. RLS / grants / RPCs
- Private `hasad` schema; RPCs in `hasad_api` INVOKER; privilege model still DRAFT pending the isolated security PoC (`07`).
- Grants: `SELECT` for `authenticated`; `INSERT` on documents/draft workflow; **no UPDATE/DELETE on posted rows** (three layers); allocation table: `SELECT`+`INSERT` only (status flips happen inside RPC transactions with audit); reconciliation: INSERT/UPDATE workflow tables only.
- Every RPC re-derives company membership and permission server-side; client-supplied party/account/company values are validated, never trusted.
- **Employee-kind documents (audit batch 2):** generic list/read RPCs **mask** the employee reference (no name, national ID or bank detail) unless the caller holds `payroll.view` — the single privacy definition remains `features/11` C13; this feature creates **no new grant on any payroll table**. Posting an employee-kind payment uses the ordinary `payment_post` (server rederives everything; C12 capacity check inside); which catalogue role holds it alongside payroll duties is the owner-pending role-catalogue item (§L).

## F. Offline SQLite and synchronization
- Receipts/payments/transfers may be drafted **offline** → pending in the outbox (D-006 OWNER_APPROVED), posted by the server in transaction order with server-issued numbers (D-013/D-018); typed rejections (closed period, inactive party/container, permission revoked per D-020) preserved locally.
- **Offline allocation entry is permitted as pending local records** (offline financial capture is owner-approved; it is not silently prohibited). Server acceptance of a pending allocation requires, at apply time, that both the payment **and** the target document are posted and that capacity/direction rules hold; a failed acceptance is returned as a typed rejection and kept locally. Recommended online-only *finalization* is a latency preference, **not** a prohibition: pending local entry vs server acceptance are explicitly distinct states.
- Reconciliation is online-only (bank data is inherently online; no offline reconciliation entry in v1 — RECOMMENDED).
- Cached container balances and unapplied credits on the client are last-server-snapshot UX, excluded from freshness guarantees while operations are pending (08 contract).

## J. Tests and acceptance criteria
Positive: receipt posts GL (DR container / CR AR+party) and gets number 1; partial allocation leaves derived open capacity; overpayment remainder appears in `unapplied_credits`; supplier payment and refund pair; transfer = one balanced two-sided entry; reversal with active allocations restores capacity and flags them reversed (append-only rows); supersede correction preserves history; reconciliation close reports matched/unmatched/variance correctly; offline receipt syncs in transaction order; **offline-entered pending allocation is accepted after its invoice posts** and rejected-with-preservation if capacity vanished. **Batch-2 additions:** a payment partially allocated to a **posted credit expense** (e.g., gross 2,360.00, allocation 1,000.00) leaves derived open 1,360.00 and each entry balances (DR AP / CR container; ΣDR = ΣCR); a **net-wage payment** after advance settlement posts DR `PAYROLL_CLEARING` (N − X, employee dimension) / CR container and the employee's clearing nets to exactly zero across run → settlement → payment (I36); a second identical resend with the same idempotency key returns the original document/number with zero new rows.
Negative: allocation exceeding payment or target capacity; cross-party and cross-company allocation; receipt allocated to an AP document ⇒ `DIRECTION_MISMATCH`; allocation against a draft target ⇒ `TARGET_NOT_POSTED`; **payment-side money allocated to a cash-mode expense ⇒ rejected (no AP movement and no payment row exist for it — target-kind + direction validation); receipt-side money allocated to an expense target ⇒ `DIRECTION_MISMATCH`; over-allocation of an expense target ⇒ `ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS`; employee-kind document carrying `party_id` (or supplier payment carrying `employee_id`) ⇒ exactly-one CHECK rejection; salary payment exceeding derived unpaid clearing (or before any posted run) ⇒ `PAYROLL_CLEARING_EXCEEDED`; any employee-kind payment entry containing an `AP_CONTROL` or party line ⇒ template test failure (I12/I36 safeguard)**; UPDATE/DELETE on posted documents ⇒ three-layer rejection; reversal of reversal; transfer to the same container; posting into a closed period; suspended member post (D-020 recheck); reused idempotency key with different payload; `anon` everywhere; **cross-company `employee_id`/expense-target refs ⇒ composite same-company FK rejection; employee reference exposed through a generic list RPC without `payroll.view` ⇒ masking test failure**.
**Concurrency:** two simultaneous `allocation_set` on one invoice — serialized per party lock, capacity never exceeded; two `payment_post` racing numbering — unique numbers; payment post racing its own reversal; delayed-ack replay. **Batch-2 additions:** two simultaneous **net-wage payments** for one employee ⇒ serialize on the per-employee advisory lock — one succeeds, one `PAYROLL_CLEARING_EXCEEDED`; duplicate replay racing the original ⇒ single execution; payment racing its own reversal ⇒ single outcome; an allocation racing the target expense's reversal ⇒ capacity restored or exceeded never (I13/I15 under the per-party lock).
**Reconciliation proofs:** signed GL party AR/AP balance ≡ settlement view including credit notes, debit notes, reversals and party-dimensioned opening balances; container derived balance ≡ Σ posted lines; statement variance = unmatched items only. **Batch-2 additions:** expense settlement proof — the same dataset yields Σ AP settlements ≡ Σ credit/employee expense gross (each settled exactly once through payment + allocation, I28); payroll proof — per-employee `PAYROLL_CLEARING` derives identically from posted lines and from (runs − settlements − payments), and never shows a debit (negative) balance under any tested sequence including the documented run-reversal workflow (I36).
Test only disposable datasets (`13_TEST_STRATEGY.md`); execution belongs to the isolated PoC.
