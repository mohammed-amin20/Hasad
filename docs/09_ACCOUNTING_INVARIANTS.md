# HASAD V2 — Accounting Invariants
**Status: DRAFT - Requires accountant/business owner review**

- Each posted journal entry has balanced total debit and credit, with approved precision.
- Same tenant, allowed fiscal period, existing accounts, valid account types and posting permissions.
- Posting and corresponding stock/payment effects must be atomic in the server transaction.
- Recorded posted events are audited and corrected with linked reversals, not silent overwrites.
- GL, customer/supplier subledgers, stock valuation and trial balance must reconcile.
- Currency conversion, tax treatment, cost of goods sold and salary formulas require explicit policies; no default assumptions.
- All amounts and official numbers have an approved precision/sequence policy.

**Acceptance**: realistic month-end sample, returns, partial payments, repeat SKUs, concurrency, reverse and re-open scenarios.

## Accounting invariants I1–I11 (PROPOSED 2026-10-20 — mechanisms RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION)
Owner-approved business rules are cited by decision ID; enforcement mechanisms remain unvalidated until the isolated PoC runs.

- **I1** Σdebits = Σcredits per posted entry, enforced by a deferred constraint trigger at COMMIT (balanced postings OWNER_APPROVED via `02` module 4; trigger mechanism RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION).
- **I2** Each line has exactly one positive side; final amounts `numeric(18,2)`; HALF_UP rounding applied at document level (D-005 OWNER_APPROVED). Intermediate precision for unit prices, quantities and costing is R-07 — **not** forced to 2 decimals.
- **I3** Posted entries and lines are immutable (D-019 OWNER_APPROVED): three independent layers — rejecting triggers, absent UPDATE/DELETE grants, draft-only RLS policies (layer design RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION).
- **I4** Posting only into an **open** fiscal period whose date range contains the entry date; closed periods reject postings (D-005 OWNER_APPROVED).
- **I5** Posting only to active **leaf** accounts of the same company (control accounts via mappings), evaluated live (RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION).
- **I6** Reversal = a new linked entry with swapped amounts and mandatory reason/audit; no silent overwrites; no second reversal of the same entry (D-019 OWNER_APPROVED).
- **I7** Single currency (ILS) in v1; **no stored balance columns** — AR/AP, trial balance and stock-valuation reconciliation derive from posted lines (D-017 OWNER_APPROVED).
- **I8** Document numbers are server-issued per company + document type + fiscal year, restarting annually, consumed transactionally under advisory locks (D-013/D-018 OWNER_APPROVED). **No gap-free guarantee under all failure scenarios** — gap policy requires PoC proof (RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION).
- **I9** Entry, account, period, party and document references always share one company (composite same-tenant FKs + RLS) — tenant boundary requirement OWNER_APPROVED (D-003 context); mechanism pending security validation.
- **I10** Opening and closing entries are ordinary balanced entries distinguished only by `entry_kind` — no integrity bypass paths (year-end **automation itself** REQUIRES_OWNER_DECISION, O2).
- **I11** Every posting transaction atomically rechecks company membership, permission and period status server-side (approved shared requirements 1/2/6); idempotency keys prevent duplicate posting (requirement 13; mechanism RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION).

## Parties, cash & allocation invariants I12–I17 (PROPOSED 2026-10-20 — mechanisms RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION)
- **I12 (AR/AP separation):** AR and AP are independent even for one unified party (D-016 OWNER_APPROVED): AR lives only on `AR_CONTROL` lines, AP only on `AP_CONTROL` lines; no query/report/RPC nets them; a receipt can never reduce AP nor a payment reduce AR (posting templates and direction rules enforce this; `DIRECTION_MISMATCH` at allocation).
- **I13 (Allocation capacity and direction):** allocations are append-only records (no mutation/deletion; corrections append supersession rows, reversals append reversal rows — RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION mechanics); Σ active allocations of a payment ≤ its amount; Σ active allocations against a target document ≤ its remaining open amount (**derived** from the posted entry and active allocations — never stored, D-017); direction compatibility enforced (AR-debit documents — sales invoices, debit notes — receive receipt-side money; AP-credit documents — purchase bills, supplier credit notes, **and credit/employee-settlement expense documents (I28; `target_kind` += `expense_document`, features/09 C6, audit batch 2)** — receive payment-side money; **cash-mode expenses are structurally not targets** — no AP movement, no payment row); concurrent `allocation_set` calls serialize per (company, party) so capacities can never be exceeded — proof is a required concurrency test; **employee-kind net-wage payments create no allocation rows** — they settle `PAYROLL_CLEARING` directly (I36).
- **I14 (Derived party-balance reconciliation):** signed GL party balance and document settlement view are **distinct measures** (D-017 OWNER_APPROVED — no stored balances). The reconciliation proof must include credit notes, debit notes, reversal pairs and party-dimensioned opening-balance lines; the simplified equation "AR = open invoices − unapplied receipts" is **not assumed to hold** — divergence cases are demonstrated by test.
- **I15 (Reversal consistency):** reversing a money document posts a linked swapped entry (D-019 OWNER_APPROVED) and, in the same transaction, reverses its active allocations — restoring target capacity and removing its unapplied credit; posted documents and allocation history rows are never mutated.
- **I16 (Transfer balance):** a cash/bank transfer is one balanced journal entry (DR destination container, CR source container) posted atomically — both sides or neither; same-container transfers rejected (`SAME_ACCOUNT`).
- **I17 (Container integrity):** every posted money document's container resolves to an active postable leaf GL account of the same company at posting time; container balances derive solely from posted lines (D-017).

## Inventory invariants I18–I21 (PROPOSED 2026-10-20 — pool/precision choices pending owner decisions)
- **I18 (Stock ledger ≡ GL):** the append-only `stock_movements` ledger is the authoritative historical record; `stock_levels` and every valuation figure are rebuildable caches. **Σ rounded movement `value` (in − out) ≡ `INVENTORY_CONTROL` GL balance by construction** (the GL amount of each movement *is* its rounded 2dp value). Residuals versus unrounded qty × current average are **disclosed by a tested residual bound, never claimed to vanish** — no claim that independently rounded movement values reproduce an unrounded valuation total. Rounding policy (R-07: 19,4 intermediates, line HALF_UP 2dp) is RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION, not owner-approved.
- **I19 (Deterministic costing):** moving weighted average (D-014 OWNER_APPROVED) recomputes only from unrounded ledger arithmetic over movement order `(product, seq)`; replaying all movements from zero must reproduce identical quantities, averages and values — the acceptance gate. Company-wide vs per-warehouse costing pools = **O12 (REQUIRES_OWNER_DECISION)**; the recommended company-wide pool coexists with per-warehouse quantity tracking by storing `unit_cost` snapshots on every movement (warehouse value = Σ its movements' values, never qty × a re-derived average).
- **I20 (No negative stock, ever):** negative quantities prohibited (D-014 OWNER_APPROVED) — enforced by in-transaction stock-row check, row-lock serialization of concurrent issues, and `CHECK (qty >= 0)`; the invariant holds at **every ledger prefix** (property-style test); negative inventory **value** is equally prohibited (unit cost ≥ 0, count implying negative on-hand rejected — O12 policy).
- **I21 (Atomicity, immutability, transfer neutrality):** a business posting's GL **and** inventory effects are one atomic server transaction rechecking membership, permission and open period (04-I11); posted movements are never updated/deleted (three layers, D-019); server-issued numbers only after successful posting (D-018); transfers preserve per-warehouse quantity and total inventory value with **zero GL lines** under company-wide pools (value-neutral under either pool choice); offline pending movements never reserve or alter authoritative stock (D-006/D-025 pending display decision); cost inputs are distinct — net-of-recoverable-VAT price only in v1, nonrecoverable VAT/freight/duties explicitly excluded pending landed-cost decisions.

## Trade invariants I22–I26 (PROPOSED 2026-10-20 — mechanisms RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION)
- **I22 (Document ≡ lines ≡ GL):** for every posted sales/purchase document: stored header `net/tax/gross` = Σ rounded line amounts (asserted at posting, immutable afterward); the GL legs derived from it balance (ΣDR = ΣCR) and equal the header amounts — sales: `DR AR gross = CR revenue net + CR VAT_OUTPUT tax`; purchases: `CR AP gross = DR inventory/expense net ± DR/CR PURCHASE_PRICE_VARIANCE + DR VAT_INPUT tax`; the document's AR/AP subledger movement equals its gross (ties to I14). Header totals are document facts, **not stored balances** (D-017 preserved).
- **I23 (Fulfillment and return capacities):** all capacities are **derived from posted children** (no stored authoritative counters): cumulative invoiced qty per order line ≤ ordered qty; cumulative returned qty per original line ≤ original line qty; credit/debit notes must reference posted originals; one-level reversal only (`ALREADY_REVERSED`); corrections append linked documents, never mutate posted rows (D-019). Concurrent capacity consumers serialize on `FOR UPDATE` of the source document so capacities can never be exceeded — proof required as a concurrency test.
- **I24 (Cash settlement single-path):** cash sales/purchases settle **only** via a linked `payment_document` + 100% `payment_allocations` row posted **in the same atomic transaction** as the business document; invoice/bill entries contain **no cash-account lines**; the AR/AP movement of the document is cancelled exactly once by the payment — double-counting a payment is structurally impossible. Refunds/overpayments follow `features/09` (O10/O22 pending).
- **I25 (Purchase-clearing reconciliation):** goods receipts post `DR INVENTORY_CONTROL / CR PURCHASE_CLEARING` (equal net amounts) with every movement having its GL leg (I18 preserved); bills against receipts consume clearing **at receipt valuation**, posting bill-vs-receipt price differences to `PURCHASE_PRICE_VARIANCE` (O18 pending owner/accountant validation); derived `PURCHASE_CLEARING` balance ≡ received-not-billed value and reaches zero for each receipt at full billing; direct purchases skip clearing in a single atomic bill+receipt transaction. - **I26 (Atomicity, idempotency, tenant isolation — trade posting):** one server transaction per business posting produces journal entry/entries + stock movements + official number + (when cash) payment + allocation together, atomically rechecking company membership, permission and open period (04-I11); idempotency keys per company make replays return the original result (`IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH` on payload change); every party/product/warehouse/account/document reference shares one company via composite same-tenant FKs (I9); client-issued numbers are never accepted (D-018); offline pending documents never affect authoritative stock or AR/AP until server acceptance in transaction order (D-006, D-025 pending).

## Payroll invariants I34–I37 (PROPOSED 2026-10-21 — mechanisms RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION; statutory content blocked by D-024)
*Scope: OWNER_APPROVED by D-015; every statutory parameter remains OPEN under D-024 — the invariants below are mechanics and identities, never legal values.*

- **I34 (Run posting balance, atomicity and immutability):** a posted payroll run produces balanced atomic GL entries through the `features/04` engine (ΣDR = ΣCR; G + E ≡ N + W + E where G = earnings+allowances, E = employer costs, W = withholdings, N = net): entry 1 debits earning/allowance/employer-cost accounts and credits `PAYROLL_CLEARING` (per-employee dimension) plus **distinct** withholding and employer-contribution payable accounts; entry 2 (same transaction) settles advances (DR `PAYROLL_CLEARING` / CR `EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES`); posted runs/lines/components are immutable (I3-class three-layer rule); the official number is server-issued at posting (D-013/D-018) and consumed transactionally (I8 semantics — rollback consumes none); the run respects strict period closing (I4/I11) and idempotent replay (I31) returns the identical run and number with zero new rows. Header totals ≡ Σ line totals ≡ Σ component details (I28-analogous). **Statutory gate: no run may post unless complete effective-dated statutory configuration exists for its period (I37).**
- **I35 (Advance integrity — assets until validly settled):** salary advances are asset-posted (`EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES`) at creation and **never duplicate salary expense** — the settlement entry touches only `PAYROLL_CLEARING` and `EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES`, never any expense account; an advance's open amount is **derived** (amount − Σ active settlements; no stored balance, D-017); Σ active settlements ≤ advance amount at all times, including under concurrent runs (per-employee advisory-lock serialization — proof is a required test, I13-analogous); settlement rows are append-only (corrections/reversals flip status with audit, never mutate or delete); reversal of a run restores advance capacity in the same transaction; an advance with active settlements cannot be reversed (`ADVANCE_IN_USE`).
- **I36 (Clearing derivation, payment exactly-once, AP separation):** per-employee `PAYROLL_CLEARING` balances derive solely from posted lines (D-017): Σ credit (net wages) − Σ debit (settlements + payments); a full cycle (run → settlement → payment of N − X) reconciles each employee's clearing balance to **exactly zero**; a derived **debit (negative) balance is never permitted**: each employee-kind payment must be `≤` the derived unpaid clearing capacity (`PAYROLL_CLEARING_EXCEEDED`), serialized per employee by an advisory lock so concurrent payments can never overdraw (proof is a required test); **employee net payments reduce `PAYROLL_CLEARING` exactly once** (idempotency key + capacity discipline — I31/I13-analogous); **no payroll posting ever writes an `AP_CONTROL` line or a party dimension** — payroll liabilities are structurally separate from supplier AP (I12 untouched; the payment vehicle is an employee-kind `payment_documents` row **specified in `features/09` C2/C12 — audit batch 2**); **run-reversal guard (recommended default, owner/accountant approval required):** a run reversal that would drive any affected employee's derived clearing balance negative is rejected (`RUN_REVERSAL_BLOCKED`) — the required workflow reverses the net payment(s) first; the alternative (tolerating negative clearing with employee repayment) is **not assumed** and `features/11` C11 wording alignment is a recorded follow-up; statutory withholdings and employer contributions remain **distinct obligations** on distinct payable accounts — never netted, never merged, never paid to the employee.
- **I37 (Statutory-configuration posting gate):** `payroll_run_post` refuses to post while the effective-dated statutory configuration required by the run's period is incomplete (`STATUTORY_CONFIG_INCOMPLETE`) — the gate evaluates the configuration **in effect on the run date, server-side**, and freezes a `config_snapshot` on the posted run so later configuration changes never rewrite history; **no statutory rate, bracket, contribution, minimum wage, entitlement or deduction limit is seeded or assumed before D-024** — until the owner answers, every post attempt failing this gate is the correct expected behavior, and draft/calculation workflows remain fully exercisable.

## Cross-module settlement scenarios (audit batch 2 — documentation-level checks; mechanisms RECOMMENDED_PENDING_VALIDATION)
Six integration scenarios verified on paper against I12–I17, I27–I28 and I34–I37. All amounts exact cents; any tax rate shown is **illustrative only (D-023 OPEN)**; all payroll rates are illustrative and **nothing is statutory (D-024)**. Each scenario becomes an executable test in the isolated PoC; none is claimed VERIFIED.

**Scenario 1 — partial settlement of an unpaid operating expense.** Credit rent expense 2,000.00 net + 360.00 illustrative VAT → gross 2,360.00 (features/10 §G): recognition entry `DR rent 2,000.00 + DR VAT_INPUT 360.00 / CR AP_CONTROL (party) 2,360.00` — ΣDR = ΣCR ✓. Settlement payment 1,000.00: `DR AP_CONTROL (party) 1,000.00 / CR bank 1,000.00` ✓ + one active allocation 1,000.00. Derived open = 2,360.00 − 1,000.00 = **1,360.00** (posted entry − Σ active allocations; never stored — D-017). Over-allocation (allocating 1,361.00) ⇒ `ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS` — forbidden (I13). Reversing the payment: swapped entry + allocation reversal in the same transaction ⇒ capacity restored to 2,360.00 (I15). Cash-mode expenses are structurally excluded from this flow (no AP movement, no payment row — features/10 C3).

**Scenario 2 — full salary settlement after payroll advance deduction.** Illustrative run (features/11 §G worked example): entry 1 `DR earnings 5,300.00 + DR employer cost 525.00 / CR PAYROLL_CLEARING 4,525.00 + CR withholding payables 775.00 + CR employer payable 525.00` — ΣDR = ΣCR = 5,825.00 ✓ and G + E ≡ N + W + E ✓ (I34); entry 2 (same transaction) advance settlement `DR PAYROLL_CLEARING 1,000.00 / CR EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES 1,000.00` ✓ (I35); net payment `DR PAYROLL_CLEARING 3,525.00 / CR bank 3,525.00` (employee-kind vehicle, features/09 C12) ✓. Per-employee clearing: 4,525.00 − 1,000.00 − 3,525.00 = **exactly zero** (I36). **No `AP_CONTROL` line and no party dimension exists anywhere in the cycle** (I12 untouched); withholdings remain on their distinct payable accounts, never netted.

**Scenario 3 — two concurrent salary payment attempts.** Employee's derived unpaid capacity: 3,525.00 (as scenario 2). Two devices submit net-wage payments of 3,525.00 with different idempotency keys: both RPCs take the **per-employee advisory lock** and serialize — the first derives capacity 3,525.00, posts (`DR clearing 3,525.00 / CR bank 3,525.00`), capacity becomes 0.00; the second rederives **0.00** ⇒ `PAYROLL_CLEARING_EXCEEDED` (zero writes beyond its rejection record). Derived clearing never shows a debit balance (I36). Same-key replay racing the original ⇒ single execution, byte-identical outcome (I31). Partial variant: 2,000.00 + 1,525.00 both succeed in lock order, Σ = 3,525.00 ≤ capacity; 2,000.00 + 2,000.00 ⇒ second rejected.

**Scenario 4 — payroll run reversal after partial salary payment (policy, not silent arithmetic).** Run posted (net 4,525.00 credited to clearing); a partial net payment of 2,000.00 posted (`DR clearing 2,000.00 / CR bank`). Derived clearing = 2,525.00. A naive run reversal (entry-1 swap debits clearing 4,525.00) would drive clearing to **−2,000.00 — a silent negative liability; never permitted**. **Policy (recommended default — owner/accountant approval required, features/09 §L):** the run-reversal RPC rejects with `RUN_REVERSAL_BLOCKED` while any affected employee's derived clearing balance < the reversal's clearing debit; the required workflow is: reverse the 2,000.00 payment first (linked swapped entry `DR bank / CR clearing 2,000.00` — capacity restored to 4,525.00; no allocations involved), then reverse the run normally (settlement rows flipped in the same transaction — features/11 C11 mechanics). The alternative policy (allow negative clearing, employee repays) is **explicitly not assumed** and would require a recorded owner/accountant decision. Under the recommended policy, every prefix of the sequence keeps clearing ≥ 0 and the trial balance Σ = 0.

**Scenario 5 — expense reversal after allocation.** Credit expense posted (AP 2,360.00) with a 1,000.00 active allocation. `expense_reverse` posts the linked swapped entry (`DR AP_CONTROL 2,360.00 / CR expense 2,000.00 + CR VAT_INPUT 360.00`, open period required) **and in the same transaction appends reversal rows for the document's active allocations** (I15 symmetry — features/10 §G): the 1,000.00 allocation becomes `reversed` (append-only history preserved), the payment retains an unapplied credit of 1,000.00 (features/09 C5, O10 recommended default), and derived AP movement nets to zero. Double reversal of the expense or the payment ⇒ `ALREADY_REVERSED`. Reversing the payment first instead yields the same integrity (capacity and unapplied credit both restore) — both orders are safe; the dedup guard-row release mechanism remains documented-but-unvalidated (features/10 C11).

**Scenario 6 — tenant isolation and payroll-detail permission enforcement.** A company-B `payment_documents` row referencing a company-A employee ⇒ composite same-company FK rejection; a company-B allocation targeting a company-A expense document ⇒ same (real per-kind FK columns, features/09 D); a forged `company_id` in the payment RPC payload ⇒ RPC rederives membership from live rows (R-03) and returns own-company-only or `PERMISSION_DENIED`. Employee-kind documents appear in generic cash list RPCs **masked** (no name, national ID or bank detail) without `payroll.view` (features/09 §E; single definition features/11 C13); report #13 per-employee detail without `payroll.view` ⇒ zero rows (I30); direct `SELECT` on payroll tables as `authenticated` ⇒ denied (no grants); any unmasked read writes its audit event; `anon` everywhere ⇒ denied.
